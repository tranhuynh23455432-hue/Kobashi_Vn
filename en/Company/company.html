Company Profile

VISION

Greeting

LIFE IS PART OF A GREATER STORY
WOVEN TOGETHER BY EVERY PERSON

KOBASHI began in 2002 in Hirakata City, Japan, under the name Kobashi Denko.
From those first steps, through steady dedication and continuous effort, we formally established Kobashi Denko Co., Ltd. in 2013.
In 2022, with the ambition of reaching into broader fields, we renamed the company KOBASHI Co., Ltd., marking a new turning point in our journey of growth.
Centered on the Kansai region and spanning from Hokkaido to Okinawa, we have continued to expand internationally, establishing our Vietnam branch in July 2025.

Everything KOBASHI has achieved today is rooted in the understanding and support of our employees' families, the dedication of every staff member, and above all, the trust and partnership of our valued customers and business partners. We would like to extend our heartfelt gratitude to everyone who has walked, and continues to walk, this journey together with KOBASHI.

We believe that a company's mission does not end with economic success. The value a business creates also lies in its contributions to society, in its capacity to support people's lives, and in its role in building a better future.

Every person is part of the greater story called life. The effort and passion we put into our work today form the foundation for the lasting value we create for tomorrow.

KOBASHI will keep searching for ways to do better, so that this story becomes ever more meaningful and vibrant. We hope to carry forward the values built by the generations before us, while opening new opportunities for the generations to come to continue shining.
With a spirit of constant innovation, ever-improving technical capability, and the pursuit of new possibilities, KOBASHI is committed to remaining a trusted partner to our customers, our business partners, and our community as we work together to build a better future.
