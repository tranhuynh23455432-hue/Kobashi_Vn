MAST Cooler

(Next-Generation Mist Cooling System)

"A new standard in cooling: mist-based air cooling that leaves nothing wet"

MAST Cooler is a mist cooling system that uses patented technology to vaporize water, releasing cool air without leaving surfaces wet.
By harnessing the latent heat of vaporization from ultra-fine water mist and generating a cool airflow through proprietary technology, combined with a high-powered electric fan to project it outward, the unit delivers cooling performance over large areas, at long distances, and even outdoors.

α-HT

(α-HT Energy-Saving Device for Air Conditioning)

"Simply connect it to the refrigerant piping!" Up to 30% energy savings

When installed on the piping, α-HT vigorously agitates the refrigerant, reducing the load on the compressor and thereby cutting power consumption.
※The compressor accounts for roughly 90% of an air conditioner's power consumption. The product has already been installed at a wide range of facilities, including factories, hospitals, and cold storage warehouses.
