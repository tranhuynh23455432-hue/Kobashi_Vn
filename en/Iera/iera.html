KOBASHI's Distinctive Strength:
In-House Design & Custom-Engineered Installation

We deliver flexible cold chain solutions, providing optimized layout drawings tailored to each facility's scale and process requirements. KOBASHI is fully equipped to handle custom, made-to-order specifications.
In parallel, we continuously pursue R&D at our in-house test laboratory to keep advancing and refining our freezing technology.

The old assumption that "frozen food loses flavor and texture"
is now completely outdated

By applying ultra-rapid freezing technology, food passes through the maximum ice crystal formation zone (-1℃ to -5℃) in a very short time. This preserves the integrity of the cell membrane structure, retaining the food's original freshness, flavor, and appearance.
